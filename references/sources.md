# References

The following public guidance supports the general phishing-awareness concepts discussed in this project. The five email examples and their classifications are original fictional training content, not sourced from these pages.

1. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA). **Recognize and Report Phishing.** https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/recognize-and-report-phishing
2. Federal Trade Commission (FTC). **How To Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams.** https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams
3. National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC). **Phishing: Spot and report scam emails, texts, websites and calls.** https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/phishing-scams
4. Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). **Business Email Compromise.** https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/scams-and-safety/common-scams-and-crimes/business-email-compromise

## Source-use note
These references are general awareness resources. They do not validate the fictional sample messages, and no claim is made that the samples were tested against live threat intelligence or technical email-authentication systems.
